Who does what at Buddhi Labs

What each person owns, and who to ask when you need an answer.

Bikash Kadayat

Founder & CEO

  • Strategy
  • Product direction
  • Client partnerships

Bikash Kadayat founded Buddhi Labs to build software around the operational problems organizations in Nepal deal with every day: keeping employee, attendance, leave and payroll information organized, and making sense of risk across electric vehicle operations.

He sets the direction for both products, Buddhi Labs HRMS and the EV Risk Intelligence System, and stays close to how customers use them so that the roadmap reflects real workflows rather than feature lists. On service projects he is involved from the first conversation through scoping, so that what is promised in writing is what gets delivered.

What Bikash is responsible for

  • Company vision, strategy and long-term planning
  • Product roadmap for HRMS and EV Risk Intelligence
  • Key client relationships, partnerships and resellers
  • Engineering standards, security and documentation practices

Best person to ask about

Product roadmap, partnerships and large implementations. Messages reach the team through the contact form and are routed to the right person.

Chief Operations Manager

  • Delivery coordination

Nabraj Kadayat keeps the day-to-day operations of Buddhi Labs running: coordinating delivery across projects, improving internal processes and making sure clients have a clear point of contact from kickoff to handover.

Nirmal B.K

Full Stack Developer

  • HRMS development
  • Integrations
  • Data pipelines

Nirmal B.K is the engineer who knows Buddhi Labs HRMS end to end. He maintains the application across its modules, from attendance and leave to tasks, documents and assets, handles the subscription logic, and turns onboarding feedback into fixes that reach customers quickly.

He also builds the backend and integration layer: payroll rules, biometric and attendance device integrations, the data pipelines that feed the EV Risk Intelligence System, and the custom systems delivered to clients. Having one engineer across both products keeps the code consistent and handovers short.

What Nirmal is responsible for

  • Buddhi Labs HRMS development, bug fixes and releases
  • Subscription, billing and onboarding-related fixes
  • Payroll rules and biometric or attendance device integrations
  • Data pipelines for EV Risk Intelligence and client software projects

Best person to ask about

Technical questions about HRMS, integrations with your existing systems and custom development. Messages reach the team through the contact form and are routed to the right person.

Ram Chandra KC

UI/UX Designer & Customer Success

  • UI/UX design
  • Customer onboarding
  • Training & support

Ram Chandra KC is the link between how Buddhi Labs products look and how customers experience them. He designs the interfaces of Buddhi Labs HRMS, the EV Risk Intelligence System and the website so that the three stay consistent, and produces the visuals used in marketing, demos and product presentations.

Because he also onboards every new HRMS customer, runs the support desk during business hours and delivers IT training sessions, the questions users ask go straight back into the design. Screens get simpler because the person who drew them is the one explaining them.

What Ram is responsible for

  • Interface design and consistency across HRMS, EV Risk Intelligence and the website
  • Marketing visuals, demo material and product presentations
  • Onboarding of every new HRMS customer, including configuration and data import
  • Support desk from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM and IT training sessions

Best person to ask about

Onboarding, support requests, training sessions and design feedback. Messages reach the team through the contact form and are routed to the right person.
