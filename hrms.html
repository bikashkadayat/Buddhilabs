Overview

One organized system for day-to-day HR operations

Buddhi Labs HRMS is designed to help organizations simplify day-to-day HR operations. It brings employee information, attendance, leave management, payroll processes, task tracking, internal documents, asset records and reporting together in one centralized system.

It is suited to organizations that want a structured, consistent way to manage people-related records and processes, with access controlled by role and reports available when decisions need to be made.

Who is HRMS for?

Teams that manage people and records

  • SMEsGrowing companies moving beyond spreadsheets and paper registers.
  • Larger organizationsMulti-department or multi-branch operations that need consistency.
  • HR and admin teamsTeams handling records, leave requests, approvals and payroll inputs.
  • ManagementLeaders who want workforce, attendance and payroll reports on demand.
HR challenges solved

Common problems HRMS is designed to address

  • Scattered employee information. Records, documents and employment details live in many files and folders.
  • Unclear attendance and leave records. Balances are hard to track and approvals happen over email or on paper.
  • Time-consuming payroll preparation. Attendance, leave and salary inputs have to be gathered and reconciled manually each month.
  • Limited reporting. Basic workforce questions take days to answer because data has to be assembled by hand.
  • Everyone sees everything, or nothing. Without role-based access it is hard to give people the right level of visibility.
What changes with HRMS

A single, structured source of HR information

  • Centralized recordsEmployee, attendance, leave and payroll information is kept in one system with a consistent structure.
  • Defined workflowsLeave requests, approvals and payroll preparation follow a repeatable process.
  • Reports when neededWorkforce, attendance, leave and payroll reports can be generated without manual assembly.
  • Access by roleAdministrators, HR teams, managers and employees each see what is relevant to them.
Key features

What Buddhi Labs HRMS includes

Employee Management

Maintain organized employee profiles, departments, designations, documents, and employment information.

Attendance Management

Track attendance records and create a structured process for attendance monitoring.

Leave and Holiday Management

Manage leave types, leave balances, holidays, requests, and approval workflows.

Payroll Management

Support structured payroll processing, salary records, payslip preparation, and related reporting.

Role-Based Access

Control access for administrators, HR teams, managers, and employees based on their responsibilities.

Reports and Insights

Generate useful workforce, attendance, leave, and payroll reports to support informed decisions.

Employee Self-Service

Enable employees to view and update permitted information, apply for leave, and access relevant HR records.

Integration Ready

Keep the platform ready for future integration with biometric devices, payroll processes, and other business systems.

Task Management

Create and assign tasks, track progress on a To Do to Done board, and see what is waiting on whom with due dates, reviews and sign-offs.

Memos, Minutes & Circulars

Draft, route and sign off memos, meeting minutes and circulars. Unfinished drafts are autosaved so nothing is lost.

Asset Management

Keep a full asset register and handle requests, assignment, transfers, returns, maintenance, exit clearance and disposal.

Nepali Calendar (B.S.)

Bikram Sambat and Gregorian dates side by side, with a built-in Nepali calendar for planning leave and deadlines.

Key benefits

Why organizations choose an HRMS

  • Less manual work. Structured processes reduce repeated data entry and reconciliation.
  • Better record keeping. Employee, attendance, leave and payroll records are kept consistently in one place.
  • Faster payroll preparation. Attendance and leave inputs are available where payroll is prepared.
  • Informed decisions. Reports give HR and management a clearer view of the workforce.
  • Clear access and accountability. Role-based access defines who can view and change information.
  • An improved employee experience. Self-service lets employees see their own records and apply for leave without chasing HR.
How HRMS works

From setup to everyday use

A typical implementation follows four stages. Timelines depend on organization size and scope.

  1. 1

    Set up your organization

    Departments, designations, leave types, holidays and salary structures are configured to match your policies.

  2. 2

    Add employee records

    Employee profiles and documents are added or imported, and roles are assigned.

  3. 3

    Run daily HR processes

    Attendance, leave requests, approvals and payroll preparation follow the defined workflows.

  4. 4

    Review reports and refine

    Workforce, attendance, leave and payroll reports support ongoing review and improvement.
