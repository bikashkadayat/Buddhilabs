# Testimonial and client logo policy

The website currently shows **no testimonials and no client logos**, by design. Nothing may be added under this policy
until the requirements below are met. Fabricated or unverified social proof is prohibited.

## Permission requirements
- **Testimonials:** written permission (email is sufficient) from the named person, confirming the exact quote, their
  name, role and organization, and that Buddhi Labs may publish it on the website and in marketing material.
- **Client logos:** written permission from an authorized representative of the client organization, plus the logo
  file supplied or approved by them (never traced or copied from the web).
- Permission must state whether it is time-limited and whether the organization must be notified before removal or reuse.

## Required attribution fields (all mandatory for a testimonial)
| Field | Example format |
|---|---|
| Full name | as the person wishes to be shown |
| Role / title | e.g. HR Manager |
| Organization | legal or trading name |
| Quote | verbatim, minor grammar fixes only with approval |
| Approval status | Approved / Pending / Withdrawn |
| Approved by, date, evidence | email reference or signed note, stored privately |
| Photo (optional) | only with explicit consent |

Anonymous or partly anonymous quotes ("HR Manager, manufacturing company") are allowed only if the person approved
that exact anonymized form.

## Approval process
1. Collect the quote or logo and the written permission.
2. Record the entry (fields above) in a private register kept outside the public repository.
3. Add the testimonial or logo to the site using the existing card components in the brand palette
   (`.card`, `.badge`, `.icon-box`); do not use star ratings or "5/5" graphics unless they come from a verifiable review platform.
4. Do not add aggregate ratings or review counts to JSON-LD unless they are genuine and verifiable.
5. A second person at Buddhi Labs reviews the page before publishing.

## Removal
On any request from the person or organization, remove the item within 5 business days, redeploy the site, and update
the private register with the withdrawal date. Remove immediately if a permission expires.

## Prohibited content
- Invented or "representative" quotes, stock-photo people presented as clients, AI-generated testimonials
- Logos of organizations that only evaluated a product, or of partners' clients
- Statistics such as "trusted by 500+ companies" without an auditable basis
- Any claim that a client endorses a specific outcome (cost savings, accident reduction) unless the client wrote it
