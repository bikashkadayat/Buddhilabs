# Legal review checklist – privacy.html and terms.html

Both pages are **draft templates** and carry a visible banner: "Draft document: This page requires review and
approval by Buddhi Labs before public launch." They are not legal advice. Complete this checklist with a qualified
legal adviser before removing the banner and publishing.

## 1. Company details to insert (both pages)
- [ ] Legal company name (as registered)
- [ ] Company registration number and registering authority
- [ ] Registered office address
- [ ] Official contact email for legal/privacy matters
- [ ] Phone number (privacy page contact section)
- [ ] Effective date and review date

## 2. Privacy Policy (privacy.html)
- [ ] Confirm the categories of personal data actually collected (form fields, server logs, analytics)
- [ ] Confirm the basis for processing wording against applicable law in Nepal and any other markets served
- [ ] List all third-party services in use: hosting provider, form provider (Formspree/Netlify/own backend), email provider, Google Analytics / Tag Manager, reCAPTCHA, Clarity/Meta Pixel if enabled
- [ ] Define data retention periods for enquiries and logs
- [ ] Cookie policy: which cookies are set, purposes, and whether a consent banner is required for your audience
- [ ] Review the implemented consent banner (accept / reject / manage, analytics off by default, choice stored in the
      visitor's browser) against the requirements of each target market; confirm the wording in privacy.html section 7
- [ ] Data subject rights procedure and response time
- [ ] International transfer statement if data is stored outside Nepal
- [ ] Children's data statement
- [ ] Separate data processing terms for HRMS and EV Risk Intelligence customers (product agreements)

## 3. Terms of Service (terms.html)
- [ ] Acceptance and scope wording
- [ ] User obligations and prohibited uses
- [ ] Intellectual property: confirm ownership of logo, product names and content
- [ ] Products and services clause: confirm that subscriptions/engagements are governed by separate written agreements
- [ ] Enquiries clause: no contract formed by submitting a form
- [ ] Accuracy of content / no-warranty wording
- [ ] Third-party links and services
- [ ] Limitation of liability wording permitted under applicable law
- [ ] Governing law and jurisdiction (expected: Nepal / Kathmandu courts – confirm)
- [ ] Changes clause
