Answers before you get in touch

The questions organizations ask most often about our products, pricing and how an engagement works.

Ask something else
What does Buddhi Labs offer?

Two SaaS products, Buddhi Labs HRMS and the EV Risk Intelligence System, and four service lines: software development, IT support, SEO and IT training. Products are subscribed to or implemented; services are scoped per project.

How much does Buddhi Labs HRMS cost?

HRMS is available on subscription: NPR 999 per month, NPR 2,599 for 3 months, NPR 4,999 for 6 months or NPR 9,990 for 12 months. Every plan includes the full product. EV Risk Intelligence pricing is provided on request.

Can we see the HRMS before subscribing?

Yes. Request a demo and we will walk through attendance, leave, tasks, documents and assets using examples from your organization, then help you choose a plan.

Do you build custom software as well?

Yes. We build web applications, business systems and integrations around your workflow, with scope, timeline and pricing agreed in writing before work starts.

Where is Buddhi Labs based and when can we reach you?

We are based in New Baneshwar, Kathmandu, Nepal. The team is available from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM by phone, WhatsApp or email, and the contact form is open at any time.

How do we get started?

Use the contact form and choose the topic closest to your need. We will reply with next steps: a demo for products, or a short discovery call for services.
