Overview

Structured visibility across EV operations

Buddhi Labs EV Risk Intelligence System is designed to help organizations organize vehicle risk information, identify operational patterns, monitor relevant events, and support better decisions across EV operations.

The system brings together information from the data sources available to your organization and presents it through dashboards, scoring, alerts and reports. Capabilities are subject to available data sources and implementation scope.

Organizations with a stake in EV operations

  • EV fleet operators
  • Logistics organizations
  • Insurance companies
  • EV manufacturers
  • Charging network operators
  • Financial institutions
  • Government and mobility organizations
EV risk challenges

Why EV risk is hard to see clearly

  • Information is scattered. Vehicle, operational, maintenance and incident data often sit in separate tools and spreadsheets.
  • Patterns go unnoticed. Without structured analysis, recurring risk indicators are difficult to identify across a fleet.
  • Priorities are unclear. Teams lack a consistent way to assess which vehicles or events need attention first.
  • Reporting takes effort. Preparing fleet-level and vehicle-level summaries for management or partners is manual work.
  • Different stakeholders need different views. Operators, insurers and finance teams look at the same fleet from different angles.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What data does the EV Risk Intelligence System use?

The system works with the data sources your organization can make available, such as fleet records, operational logs, maintenance information or partner data. Data coverage and update frequency are subject to available data sources and implementation scope.

Does the system predict battery failures or prevent accidents?

No. The system is designed to help organize risk information, apply configurable scoring and support decisions. It does not guarantee prediction of failures, prevention of incidents or specific cost or insurance outcomes.

Can it integrate with our fleet or insurance systems?

The platform is designed to be integration-ready. Integration with fleet systems, insurance systems or EV data sources is scoped and confirmed during implementation.

Who typically uses the system?

EV fleet operators, logistics organizations, insurance companies, EV manufacturers, charging network operators, financial institutions and government or mobility organizations.
