Frequently asked questions

How quickly will I hear back?

We aim to respond to every enquiry within a few business days. Response times may vary depending on the request.

Do you work with organizations outside Nepal?

Yes. Our SaaS products and our development, SEO and training services can be delivered remotely. On-site IT support availability depends on location and is confirmed on request.

Can I get pricing without a call?

For product subscriptions, share your employee or vehicle count and we can respond with pricing information by email. Custom projects usually need a short conversation to scope properly.
