Who we are

Products and services from one team

Buddhi Labs builds software for the operational problems organizations face every day: keeping employee, attendance, leave and payroll information organized, and making sense of risk across electric vehicle operations.

Our two SaaS products, Buddhi Labs HRMS and the EV Risk Intelligence System, are supported by a full set of services: custom software development, IT support, SEO and IT training. Because we both build and support software, our products benefit from real service experience and our services benefit from product discipline.

We are based in Kathmandu, Nepal, and work with modern engineering, security and documentation practices so that clients get a partner who understands the local context and delivers to an international standard.

Our mission

To help organizations work smarter by building practical, reliable software and delivering technology services with honesty, skill and care.

Our vision

To be a trusted technology partner for organizations in Nepal, and to take products built in Kathmandu to organizations across the region and beyond.
