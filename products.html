FAQ

Common questions about our products

Are the products offered as SaaS?

Yes. Both Buddhi Labs HRMS and the EV Risk Intelligence System are developed and offered by Buddhi Labs as software products. Deployment and access options are agreed based on implementation scope.

How is pricing structured?

Buddhi Labs HRMS is offered on subscription: NPR 999 per month, NPR 2,599 for 3 months, NPR 4,999 for 6 months or NPR 9,990 for 12 months. EV Risk Intelligence pricing is provided on request and depends on scope and implementation requirements.

Can you help with data migration and setup?

Yes. Setup, configuration and data import are part of implementation. The approach depends on the format and quality of your existing data.

What support is included?

Onboarding, user guidance and ongoing support are discussed and agreed as part of each engagement. Support hours and response expectations are confirmed in writing.
